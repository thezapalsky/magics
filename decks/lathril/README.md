# Lathril, Blade of the Elves

Released version: `0.3.2`

This paper Commander deck is primarily an Elfball deck: build an Elf board, generate mana and tokens, connect with Lathril, and threaten her ten-Elf activated ability. Graveyard recursion and Massacre Wurm are secondary tools, not the primary identity.

Current composition:

- 1 commander
- 64 nonland cards
- 35 lands
- 100 cards total

The released list remains unchanged while experiments are running. Candidate lists live under `experiments/`.

## Release 0.3.2

- Added: 1 Changeling Wayfinder, 1 Risky Research, and 1 Scarblade's Malice.
- Removed: 1 Mazemind Tome, 1 Ravenous Amulet, and 1 Basilisk Collar.
- Adopted from [EXP-002](../../experiments/EXP-002-artifact-trim/README.md) after the user confirmed completing all three physical paper swaps on 2026-09-05.

This reduces the artifact count from 13 to 10. Changeling Wayfinder adds an Elf body and a guaranteed basic-land search; Risky Research provides immediate card selection and draw while supporting the secondary graveyard package; Scarblade's Malice can turn a creature trade into a 2/2 Elf token. The land count remains 35. This is a user-directed tuning release motivated by awkward Arena mirror hands, not a verified paper play-test improvement.

## Release 0.3.1
