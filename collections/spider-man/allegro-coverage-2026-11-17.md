# Spider-Man binder: Allegro coverage snapshot

Checked 2026-11-17. Prospective purchases only; inventory remains 60/79 SPM plus eight owned SPE bonuses. No cart was edited and no cards were purchased.

## Scope and result

- All 25 currently reserved missing names were searched: 19 SPM and six optional SPE.
- Current listings or live search matches were found for all 25 names. This is coverage of the checklist, not a claim that every Allegro listing was exhaustively enumerated or that one seller holds everything.
- The 15-card Arcanum basket is inferred as the six screenshot items plus the nine later additions. Its listed item total is 283.50 PLN.
- The selected replacement offers for those same 15 names total 221.07 PLN before shipping, a 62.43 PLN item-price reduction. Cosmic Spider-Man, Ultimate Green Goblin and Doc Ock retain foil versions; Eddie Brock uses a different nonfoil artwork.
- The replacement prices span multiple sellers. Shipping and Smart eligibility were not checked in a real combined cart; they can reduce or erase the savings. Some condition/printing details remain unverified.
- Exact card names were matched. Superior Foes of Spider-Man, SPE Ghost-Spider already owned, toys, comics and art-only products were excluded.

## Scene Box alternative

[TrollsCave Scene Box offer, 19118179627](https://allegro.pl/produkt/karta-magic-the-gathering-magic-the-gathering-marvel-s-spiderman-scene-box-wizards-of-the-coast-d6fcf064-983d-49ea-aca4-24a3980864ed?offerId=19118179627): 95.00 PLN, shown as new with original packaging and two units available on the live product page. The description is sparse and calls it Spectacular Showdown; the official product is Spidey's Sensational Showdown. Confirm that this is the complete sealed standard product before purchase.

The standard manufacturer product contains six traditional foil borderless scene cards, three Spider-Man Play Boosters, six art cards and a display easel. Its four legendary creatures are the four currently missing SPE Scene Box names:

- Sensational Spider-Man (SPE 0025)
- Green Goblin, Nemesis (SPE 0023)
- Venom, Deadly Devourer (SPE 0022)
- Doc Ock, Evil Inventor (SPE 0024)

The selected singles for those four total 164.84 PLN. The 95 PLN box is 69.84 PLN lower in item cost and also includes three Play Boosters, assuming the advertised product is the complete standard sealed box. Replace the cart's individual Doc Ock if choosing the box to avoid buying that name twice.

The selected 18 SPM offers plus the Scene Box total 355.52 PLN before delivery. That covers 22 of the 25 missing names; Morlun and the two white Welcome Deck SPE names would remain. This is a comparison scenario, not a recommended automatically optimized delivered basket.
