## Goal and scope

Collect all **79 unique Legendary Creature cards** from the main **Magic: The Gathering | Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM)** set, regardless of rarity or price. This is a curated character collection, not a competitive deck or investment project. The mandatory target remains fixed at 79; Spider-Man Eternal (SPE) cards are optional bonuses.

- Include legendary artifact creatures, Heroes, Villains and named supporting characters such as Aunt May and Flash Thompson.
- Exclude nonlegendary creatures, generic Citizens, spells, enchantments, equipment, noncreature artifacts and lands.
- Keep one copy per unique card name. A double-faced card occupies one slot.
- Choose the displayed copy in this order: **full-art/showcase artwork > foil > regular printing**.
- Put duplicates and less-preferred printings into storage; alternate artwork does not increase the collection target.
- Use black-and-white printed proxies for missing cards until acquired.
- Apply no minimum-value threshold to Spider-Man characters.

The Soul Stone is a separate potential chase card and does not belong to the 79-card Legendary Creature checklist.
