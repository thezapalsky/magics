## Goal and scope

Collect all **79 unique Legendary Creature cards** from **Magic: The Gathering | Marvel's Spider-Man (SPM)**, regardless of rarity or price. This is a curated character collection.

- Include legendary artifact creatures, Heroes, Villains and named supporting characters such as Aunt May and Flash Thompson.
- Exclude nonlegendary creatures, generic Citizens, instants, sorceries, enchantments, equipment and lands.
- Keep one copy per unique card name. A double-faced card occupies one slot.
- Prefer full-art/showcase versions over regular foils when choosing the displayed copy.
- Put identical duplicates into storage; collecting every alternate printing is outside the scope.
- Use black-and-white printed proxies for missing cards until acquired.
- Apply no minimum-value threshold to Spider-Man characters.

The Soul Stone is a separate potential chase card and does not belong to the 79-card Legendary Creature checklist.
